Currency, Exchange Rate & Quotation Terms

USD is the base pricing currency. Indicative Ethiopian Birr (ETB) equivalents are calculated using the National Bank of Ethiopia (NBE) indicative rate checked on November 24, 2026 (1 USD = 161.4163 ETB), rounded to the nearest 50 Birr.

  • The NBE indicative rate is a reference benchmark, not a guaranteed commercial bank transaction rate.
  • For new quotations, the latest official NBE indicative rate (nbe.gov.et/exchange) and quotation date are confirmed prior to project start.
  • Revisions cover adjustments within the agreed package scope; expanded requirements, extra records, or urgent delivery can be quoted individually.
