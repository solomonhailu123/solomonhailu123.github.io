Solomon Data Assistant — Architecture & Modules

Solomon Data Assistant is a personal Telegram Mini App and bot designed to streamline repetitive data operations, field survey checks, and executive reporting into a mobile-first workspace.

1. Live Operational Dashboard

Tracks synced records across KoboToolbox Field API, Google Sheets Ledgers, and Google Forms Intake with 7-day throughput velocity metrics.

2. Multi-Mode Task Execution

Supports Data Cleaning & PivotTables, Survey Data Sync (Kobo/ODK), Online Research Synthesis, Admin Word Reports, SQL Queries, and SPSS Codebooks.

3. Direct Data Connectors

Connects to KoboToolbox v2 endpoints and Google Sheets URLs to ingest raw rows, validate schemas, and sync clean outputs back to Google Drive.

4. Automated File Deliverables

Generates formatted .xlsx workbooks (with SUMIFS, XLOOKUP, and Pivot layouts) and .docx executive memos ready for download.
